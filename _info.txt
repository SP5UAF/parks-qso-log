The ADIF upload and QSO Search utilities were created to keep track of my 2026 vacation activities from WWFF, POTA and SOTA areas.
I created the first version as my personal self-education in AI programming.
After the first weeks of using I decided to implement new features - still as a way of learning AI tools.
Finally I decided to keep logs from all of my portable operations in the system and to implement new features and ideas.
